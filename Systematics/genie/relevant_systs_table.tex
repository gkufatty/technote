\subsection{Data-Driven Relevance of Neutron-Multiplicity Systematics}
\label{sec:relevantSystsDataDriven}

Table~\ref{table:neutronSysts} ranked the 2024-tune cross-section
systematics by \emph{expected} relevance, from the mechanisms established
in the sections above. To turn that expectation into a measurement, every
systematic in that ranking (77 in total, across the FSI, MEC, RES, QE, and
DIS/hadronization categories) was shifted independently by
$\pm1\sigma$ and compared against nominal in the diagnostic variable(s)
Table~\ref{table:neutronSystDiagnostics} identifies for its category
(visible-neutron kinetic energy, prong multiplicity, and the
primary-vs-visible neutron count for FSI; $E_{\mathrm{avail}}$ and the
final-state topology for MEC/RES/DIS; the clean-CCQE and inclusive
$E_{\mathrm{avail}}$ rate for QE), for both FHC and RHC. For each
(systematic, diagnostic, beam) triple the shifted spectrum was rescaled to
nominal's total and compared bin-by-bin via
$\chi^2_{\mathrm{shape}} = \sum_i (s_i-n_i)^2/n_i$, isolating genuine shape
distortion from a pure rate change.

Table~\ref{table:relevantSysts} lists the 26 systematics that reach
$\chi^2_{\mathrm{shape}} > 5$ in at least one diagnostic in at least one
beam -- a threshold chosen to keep only unambiguous, clearly-nonzero
effects rather than the marginal ($\chi^2\sim1$--$5$) middle ground, which
contributes little to the resulting covariance while roughly tripling the
size of the systematic ensemble. Of the 77 systematics evaluated, 51 fall
below this threshold everywhere and are dropped from the neutron-
multiplicity multiverse; all 9 of the LowPriority-tier systematics (COH,
NC-elastic, radiative, and 2nd-class-current knobs) are among those
dropped, confirming the expectation from
Table~\ref{table:neutronSysts} for that tier, with one near-miss worth
noting: the $\Delta$ radiative $\eta$-decay branching knob (\texttt{RDecBR1eta})
reaches $\chi^2_{\mathrm{shape}}=3.6$ in the true-multiplicity sanity check
(FHC) -- real, but still short of the cut.

Two systematics Table~\ref{table:neutronSysts} lists as highest-priority
for setting the RES target-nucleon $p$-vs-$n$ ratio
(\texttt{RESvpvnRatioNuXSecSyst}/\texttt{RESvpvnRatioNubarXSecSyst}) do not
reach this threshold in any RES diagnostic ($\chi^2_{\mathrm{shape}}\leq2.3$
throughout), nor does the $\Delta$-decay angular-distribution knob
(\texttt{Theta\_Delta2Npi}, $\chi^2_{\mathrm{shape}}\leq1.0$) -- despite the
physical mechanism each is meant to control being exactly the one this
analysis is most sensitive to. This may reflect the size of the
$\pm1\sigma$ variation itself in the 2024 tune rather than an absence of
physical impact, and is worth revisiting if that tune changes.

By category: FSI keeps all 5 systematics evaluated, MEC keeps all 6 --
both fully validate Table~\ref{table:neutronSysts}'s highest-priority
billing. RES keeps 6 of 10 (dropping the two $p$-vs-$n$ ratio knobs, the
$\Delta$-decay-angle knob, and \texttt{MvNCRES}). QE keeps 3 of 7,
dominated by \texttt{ZNormCCQE}'s rate effect (Sec.~\ref{sec:...}: a
$\pm16\%$ shift in the clean-CCQE-like sample and $\pm6$--$8\%$ in the
overall CC rate by beam). DIS keeps only 6 of 40 -- the two
hadronic-energy-scale knobs (\texttt{DISNuHadronQ1Syst}/
\texttt{DISNuBarHadronQ0Syst}) and the four largest CC multi-pion knobs;
the remaining 34 soft-pion and AGKY/Bodek-Yang hadronization knobs are
negligible in this selection, several bin-for-bin identical to nominal.
Nearly every retained systematic is far more visible in the final-state
topology or FSI confusion diagnostic than in the corresponding
multiplicity-count histogram, which is the quantitative version of this
technote's qualitative claim that the true-multiplicity histogram alone is
necessary but not sufficient (Sec.~\ref{sec:...}).
